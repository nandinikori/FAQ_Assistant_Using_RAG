# Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-014  
Version: 1.0  
Effective date: 2025-01-01

## 1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed onboarding may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Roles requiring on-site equipment or direct in-person service may have different schedules.

| Work arrangement | Weekly remote days | Approval |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Standard eligible role | 2 | Direct manager |
| On-site service role | 0 | Department head |
| Temporary accommodation | Case-specific | People Operations |

## 2. Availability and security

Employees must remain available during their team's core hours, 10:00–15:00 local time. Company devices must use the approved VPN and screen-lock policy. Confidential records must not be printed or left visible in shared spaces.

## 3. Exceptions

People Operations may approve a temporary exception for disability accommodation, caregiving disruption, or a documented site closure. A manager cannot waive security requirements. Exceptions must record an owner, reason, approval date, and review date.

## 4. Review

Managers review arrangements every six months. This policy is synthetic demonstration data and is not real employment advice.
