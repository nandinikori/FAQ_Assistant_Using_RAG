# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy ID: FIN-022  
Version: 1.0  
Effective date: 2025-07-01

## 1. Booking approval

Business travel must be approved before booking. Employees should use the preferred booking channel and select refundable fares when the additional cost is reasonable.

## 2. Reimbursement limits

The table lists daily reimbursement ceilings. Local taxes are included in the ceiling. Receipts are required for every lodging claim and for other expenses above the stated threshold.

| Region | Lodging per night | Meals per day | Receipt threshold |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Domestic | USD 220 | USD 65 | USD 25 |
| International tier A | USD 300 | USD 85 | USD 25 |
| International tier B | USD 240 | USD 70 | USD 25 |

## 3. Exceptions and appeals

A department vice president may approve an exception to a reimbursement ceiling before travel when a documented business need exists. An employee may appeal a denied expense within 30 calendar days by attaching the receipt and manager rationale.

## 4. Records

Expense reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days after the trip. Finance retains approved reports according to the corporate retention schedule.
