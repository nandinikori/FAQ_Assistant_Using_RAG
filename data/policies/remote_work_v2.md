# Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-014  
Version: 2.0  
Effective date: 2026-04-01

## 1. Eligibility

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days each week after onboarding and manager approval. Site-dependent roles follow the schedule published by their department.

| Work arrangement | Weekly remote days | Approval |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Standard eligible role | 3 | Direct manager |
| Site-dependent role | Department schedule | Department head |
| Temporary accommodation | Case-specific | People Operations |

## 2. Availability and security

Employees must be reachable during team core hours agreed in writing by the manager. Company devices must use the approved VPN and screen-lock policy. Confidential records must not be printed or left visible in shared spaces.

## 3. Exceptions

People Operations may approve a temporary exception for disability accommodation, caregiving disruption, or a documented site closure. A manager cannot waive security requirements. Exceptions must include an owner, reason, approval date, review date, and expiration date.

## 4. Version control

Version 2.0 supersedes version 1.0 from its effective date. Historical versions remain available for audit and must not be silently overwritten.
